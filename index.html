From demos to real systems

Agents are ready
for real systems.

Nobody wants to be the one who let them in.

An agent that can restart a deployment can also delete one. A bad plan, a confused model or a poisoned web page is one tool call away from production, so most agents stay in demos.

Fort gives them a place to live where every call is checked against your policy, risky ones wait for a person, and nothing leaves the walls unless you said it could.

A foundation for trust

Know the agent.
Control the action.

Fort brings identity, policy enforcement and evidence
to the agents working across your systems.

01 / Identity

Know who's acting.

Give every agent a unique identity. Know who owns it, which version is running, and who approved its actions.

02 / Control

Set the boundaries.

Define the tools and data each agent can use. Require a person to approve risky actions before they happen.

03 / Evidence

Keep the proof.

Keep a tamper-evident record of every allowed and blocked call. Evidence stays with you, in your cloud.
